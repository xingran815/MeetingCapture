## Surprises
- **The unit suite grew from about 130 s to about 390 s,** because the echo tests run real filtering on invented audio in debug. Pre-push takes that long, and a plain push then died with SIGPIPE, apparently from the idle SSH connection. A keepalive fixes it, with hooks still running (D-75).
- **CI's E2E failed once on `4289e08`.** Unexplained, see Gates.
- **The clocks differ by about 8 ppm.** Lag climbs from 144 to 148 ms over `main-3`, which is why a fixed filter stops at 8.7 dB. Correcting it gave 18.6.
- Float32 in the solve cost about 10 dB.
- **Whisper's response to leftover echo is close to chance.** The Python and Swift outputs differ only at -35.6 dB, with section levels matching to 0.2 dB, yet score 1.1% and 9.1% under `You` without the line check, and 6.2% after small later fixes. Leftover echo sits near the room, where Whisper may or may not invent words. So the line check is what holds item 3, and a word figure for a method without it means little on one run.
- The old repeat score stays over 95% with no user voice at all.
- Leaving S5 out of the other side's count moved `main-2` from 4.6% to 1.1% before the later fixes. Its `invented` count rises because those echo words now have nothing to match.
- WebRTC's build was refused by this session's safety check (D-70).
