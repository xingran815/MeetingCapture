## Still unsure
- A CLI call already under way when a job is cancelled isn't stopped (`CLIRunner`); its data has already gone. Stopping it is a subprocess change that needs a security review (deep review low, left).
- Untested: cancelling a late-aborted job that is still *queued* (the `waiting` list is private), and a successful job finishing during a recording (only a failing one can be driven without a real model).
- The shell's x+Enter doesn't ask (D-103); the Trash keeps the files.
- The abort question from the menu bar with the window closed was not checked by hand.
