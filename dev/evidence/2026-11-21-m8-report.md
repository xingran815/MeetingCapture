# M8 report: better echo removal, measured on the AEC-Challenge data

Issue 31, branch `m8-aec`. No separate spec: the issue body holds the approved goals, and the full-band goals were approved in the session (2026-11-20). Decisions: D-106, D-107.

**Status: done. The user chose the shipped weights knowing which goals are unmet (D-107). Verifier, deep review and security review ran before the push; their findings are fixed here and the deep re-check approved.** Every figure below comes from the challenge's public clips, the user's M3.2 lab takes and one real call of the user's (16 Nov). The user's recordings stayed on the Mac; only public challenge features went to the training machines.

## What landed
| Area | Now |
|---|---|
| Debug audio | A setting and flag keep the mic before and after echo removal beside the recording, for listening (d7142f4) |
| Linear stage | Aligns on the echo's first arrival, fits unshifted when no delay shows (a7fe9f2), and refits each window around the user's voice in double talk (08c7eda) |
| Residual suppressor | `EchoSuppressor`: classical per-bin gain times a neural GRU mask (geometric mean) plus comfort noise (D-106) |
| Full-band network | One 513-bin GRU (hidden 160, 0.64 M weights, half precision in `EchoSuppressorWeights.swift`) trained on synthetic 48 kHz clips, the 16 kHz challenge clips and real 48 kHz PC calls whose tracks carry sound above 8 kHz, `--real-far-only --keep 8`, float32 (D-107) |
| int16 fix | `LinearStageFile` rounds the error and estimate to 16 bits, as the training dumps are. Without it, networks trained on real calls took 20 dB off the user's voice in the app |
| Lab | `scripts/aec-bench/`: the 16 kHz bench (`eval.py`), the full-band bench (`fb_data.py`, `fb_eval.py`), real full-band data and test (`real_data.py`, `rfb_eval.py`), training (`nres.py train-mix`), export, a sweep runner, a GPU screen (`screen_data.py`, `screen.py`, not yet run on the Mac) |

## Choosing the weights
Seventeen networks plus three checks were scored on every bench, the long takes and the call (logs local in `dev/tmp/aec-challenge`). k8 seed 0 was the first to beat the previous weights (C) overall. Two more seeds and `--keep 16` (2026-11-21):

| | C | k8 s0 (shipped) | k8 s1 | k8 s2 | keep 16 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Real full-band DT echo / degradation | 4.01 / 3.20 | 4.25 / 3.12 | 4.39 / 2.91 | 4.31 / 3.03 | 4.35 / 2.79 |
| Real full-band high-band ERLE | 18.1 | 24.6 | 25.1 | 23.3 | 24.8 |
| `main-3` voice talked over | 1.32 | 0.89 | 1.00 | 1.19 | 0.85 |
| `main-3` voice alone | 0.14 | 0.11 | 0.15 | 0.19 | 0.02 |

The extra echo removal holds across seeds; part of seed 0's double-talk degradation edge is luck. The user chose seed 0.

Lows left (dev/NOW.md): the unshifted fit can take a far track carrying the user's own voice at 0-1 ms as echo; triple correlation work per refit; a periodic far side could move the first-arrival lag; on an output folder on another volume a failed debug move can leave a partial unlisted file; one assertion in the taken-name test can't fail, the spectra read-back test is weak, and the out-of-space note isn't tested end to end through `clean`; `ci.sh`, `build-app.sh` and `install.sh` don't pin the build system.

## Surprises
- The train/deploy mismatch: the app fed float features, the lab trained on int16 WAV dumps. A far-silent frame's estimate sat at a median log power of -20.8 against -17.2, and networks trained on real calls learned to rely on that floor. Proven with float dumps (Python predicted a 22 dB loss, the app gave 20).
- A shared work folder in `eval.py` let parallel runs read each other's files (ERLE 77 and 220 dB); every 16 kHz score was rerun after the fix.
- Release builds score identically to debug and run the app about 3× faster.

## Still unsure
- One seed of three; the real full-band test is 10 sessions, so a gap under about 0.1 MOS may be noise.
- The challenge's real recordings carry their own licence terms; whether trained weights inherit them is unsettled (D-106, D-107).
- `screen.py` and `screen_data.py` were written on the training machine and not run on the Mac.
- Echo removal is slower: a short CLI case takes about 19 s in a debug build.
