## Concepts to explore

- **cognitive defusion**
  - Said: Instead of 'I'm going to fail,' you notice 'I'm having the thought that I'm going to fail.'
  - AI understands: Seeing a thought as a thought, not as a fact.
  - More: Defusion is about changing your relationship to a thought, not arguing with it or pushing it away. The phrase "I'm having the thought that" marks it as a thought.  It is a skill that takes practice.
  - Source: [Defusion: an overview](https://example.org/act/cognitive-defusion)
  - Source: [Practice sheet](https://example.org/act/defusion-sheet.pdf)
- **values exercise**

  - AI understands: Naming what matters and choosing actions that follow it.
  - More: Naming values first, then choosing small actions that fit them, tends to feel less like a test than setting goals alone.
  - Source: [Values in therapy](https://example.org/act/values)
