Concepts to explore

cognitive defusion

What was said
Instead of 'I'm going to fail,' you notice 'I'm having the thought that I'm going to fail.'
What the AI understands
AI Seeing a thought as a thought, not as a fact.
More detail

Defusion is about changing your relationship to a thought, not arguing with it or pushing it away. The phrase "I'm having the thought that" marks it as a thought.

It is a skill that takes practice.
